LETTER OF CREDIT REQUIREMENTS FOR TEXTILE EXPORTS
==================================================

WHAT IS A LETTER OF CREDIT (LC)?
---------------------------------
A Letter of Credit is a bank guarantee that ensures payment
to the exporter provided all LC terms and document requirements
are met exactly. Governed by UCP 600 (Uniform Customs and Practice
for Documentary Credits — ICC Publication 600).

STANDARD DOCUMENTS REQUIRED IN TEXTILE LC SHIPMENTS
----------------------------------------------------
1. Commercial Invoice
   - Must be issued by beneficiary (exporter)
   - Description must match LC exactly — word for word
   - Amount must not exceed LC value
   - Must show LC number and date
   - Incoterm must match LC terms

2. Full Set of Bill of Lading (3/3 originals)
   - Made out to order of issuing bank (for negotiation LC)
   - Marked freight prepaid or freight collect per Incoterm
   - On board notation required — with vessel name and date
   - Notify party: usually buyer or their agent

3. Packing List
   - Must match invoice quantities exactly
   - Carton numbers, net weight, gross weight, dimensions
   - Marks and numbers must match BL

4. Certificate of Origin
   - Issued by Chamber of Commerce (FPCCI, KCCI, LCCI)
   - Form A for GSP claiming countries
   - Signed and stamped — original required
   - Description must match invoice

5. Insurance Certificate (if CIF/CIP terms)
   - Minimum 110% of invoice value
   - Institute Cargo Clauses — as specified in LC
   - Endorsed in blank if required by LC
   - Must cover port of loading to port of discharge

COMMON LC DISCREPANCIES IN TEXTILE EXPORTS
-------------------------------------------
DISCREPANCY 1: Late Presentation
- Documents presented after LC expiry date
- Documents presented after presentation period (usually 21 days from BL date)
- Solution: Ship early — present documents within 7 days of shipment

DISCREPANCY 2: Description Mismatch
- Invoice description differs from LC description even slightly
- Example: LC says "100% Cotton Jersey Fabric" — invoice says "Cotton Jersey"
- Solution: Copy LC description word-for-word onto invoice

DISCREPANCY 3: Short Shipment
- Quantity shipped less than LC quantity without tolerance clause
- LC typically allows +/- 5% tolerance on quantity and value
- Solution: Confirm tolerance clause with buyer before shipment

DISCREPANCY 4: BL Issues
- BL not marked "on board" — only "received for shipment"
- BL shows different port than LC
- BL date after latest shipment date in LC
- Solution: Confirm vessel booking before issuing BL

DISCREPANCY 5: Certificate of Origin Issues
- CO description different from invoice
- CO not authenticated properly
- Form A errors — wrong GSP code
- Solution: Prepare CO after invoice is finalised — copy description

DISCREPANCY 6: Insurance Gaps (CIF terms)
- Insurance value less than 110% of CIF value
- Wrong clauses — LC requires Clause A, exporter submits Clause C
- Solution: Always insure for 110% — check LC for clause requirement

DISCREPANCY 7: Missing Documents
- LC requires inspection certificate — not submitted
- LC requires beneficiary certificate — not submitted
- Solution: Read entire LC before shipment — prepare checklist

UCP 600 KEY RULES FOR EXPORTERS
---------------------------------
Article 14: Standard for examination of documents
- Banks have 5 banking days to examine documents
- Documents must comply on their face with LC terms

Article 16: Discrepant documents
- Issuing bank must notify discrepancies within 5 banking days
- Exporter can correct and resubmit within LC validity

Article 20: Bill of Lading requirements
- Must show name of carrier
- Must show port of loading and discharge
- Must be signed by carrier or agent
- On board notation must show date and vessel name

Article 28: Insurance documents
- Must be issued by insurance company or underwriter
- Must be dated no later than BL date
- Must be in same currency as LC

LC PAYMENT TIMELINE FOR TEXTILE EXPORTS
-----------------------------------------
At Sight LC: Payment within 5-7 banking days after document acceptance
Usance LC (30/60/90 days): Payment after usance period from BL date
Deferred Payment: Payment on specified future date

TIPS TO AVOID LC REJECTION
----------------------------
1. Request a copy of LC draft before it is opened — check all terms
2. Prepare a document checklist from LC before shipment
3. Never deviate from LC description on any document
4. Ship before latest shipment date — allow 7 days for document preparation
5. Use same units throughout — LC says metres, invoice must say metres
6. Verify beneficiary name and address matches LC exactly
7. Check if LC is transferable or not before making any assignments
