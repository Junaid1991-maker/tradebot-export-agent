INCOTERMS 2020 GUIDE FOR TEXTILE EXPORTERS
===========================================

INCOTERMS 2020 — 11 TERMS EXPLAINED

EXW — Ex Works
--------------
Risk transfers: At seller's premises
Cost responsibility: Buyer pays everything from seller's door
Documents required: Commercial Invoice, Packing List only
Textile usage: Rarely used for export — used for domestic or small orders
Note: Buyer arranges all export clearance — risky for seller

FCA — Free Carrier
------------------
Risk transfers: When goods handed to carrier at named place
Cost responsibility: Seller pays to named point, buyer pays from there
Documents required: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Export Declaration
Textile usage: Used for air freight shipments — growing usage
Note: Can use with LC — seller can request BL from carrier

CPT — Carriage Paid To
-----------------------
Risk transfers: When goods handed to first carrier
Cost responsibility: Seller pays freight to destination, buyer pays insurance
Documents required: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Freight Contract
Textile usage: Moderate use for smaller shipments
Note: Risk transfers earlier than cost responsibility

CIP — Carriage and Insurance Paid To
-------------------------------------
Risk transfers: When goods handed to first carrier
Cost responsibility: Seller pays freight AND insurance to destination
Documents required: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Insurance Certificate
Textile usage: Used when buyer requires insurance coverage
Note: Higher insurance minimum than CIF (Institute Cargo Clauses A)

DAP — Delivered at Place
------------------------
Risk transfers: At named destination, before unloading
Cost responsibility: Seller pays all except import duties
Documents required: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, all freight docs
Textile usage: Rare — used for duty-paid delivered orders
Note: Buyer handles import clearance

DPU — Delivered at Place Unloaded
-----------------------------------
Risk transfers: After unloading at destination
Cost responsibility: Seller pays all including unloading, except import duties
Documents required: Full document package
Textile usage: Very rare in textile exports
Note: Only term where seller responsible for unloading

DDP — Delivered Duty Paid
--------------------------
Risk transfers: At destination, fully cleared
Cost responsibility: Seller pays everything including import duties
Documents required: Full package + import clearance documents
Textile usage: Used for key account buyers (supermarkets) demanding DDP
Note: Most risk for seller — requires knowledge of destination duty rates
Pakistan textile DDP usage: Germany technical textiles, institutional buyers

FAS — Free Alongside Ship
--------------------------
Risk transfers: When goods placed alongside vessel at port
Cost responsibility: Seller pays to alongside ship, buyer pays loading + freight
Documents required: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Port Delivery Receipt
Textile usage: Rare — mainly bulk/break-bulk cargo

FOB — Free on Board
--------------------
Risk transfers: When goods loaded on vessel at port of shipment
Cost responsibility: Seller pays to loading on ship, buyer pays freight + insurance
Documents required: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, CO
Textile usage: MOST COMMON for Pakistani textile exports
Pakistan FOB ports: Port Qasim, Karachi Port
LC documentation: FOB terms most LC-friendly
Note: After goods on board — risk is buyer's

CFR — Cost and Freight
-----------------------
Risk transfers: When goods loaded on vessel (same as FOB)
Cost responsibility: Seller pays freight to destination port, buyer pays insurance
Documents required: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, CO, Freight Invoice
Textile usage: Common for UAE, Saudi Arabia, Australia shipments
Note: Risk transfers at loading even though seller pays freight

CIF — Cost Insurance and Freight
----------------------------------
Risk transfers: When goods loaded on vessel (same as FOB)
Cost responsibility: Seller pays freight AND insurance to destination port
Documents required: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, CO, Insurance Certificate
Textile usage: Common for European and UK buyers preferring CIF
Note: Seller arranges insurance but risk still transfers at loading
Insurance minimum: 110% of CIF value — Institute Cargo Clauses C minimum

INCOTERM DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS SUMMARY
---------------------------------------
FOB: Commercial Invoice + Packing List + Bill of Lading + Certificate of Origin
CIF: All FOB docs + Insurance Certificate + Freight Invoice
CFR: All FOB docs + Freight Invoice
CIP: All CPT docs + Insurance Certificate (Clause A)
DDP: Full package + Import Clearance + Duty Payment Proof
