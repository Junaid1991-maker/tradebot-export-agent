TRADE AGREEMENTS AND GSP BENEFITS FOR PAKISTAN TEXTILE EXPORTS
===============================================================

PAKISTAN GSP STATUS OVERVIEW
------------------------------
EU (European Union): GSP+ — Enhanced preferences — ACTIVE
UK (United Kingdom): DCTS Enhanced Framework — ACTIVE
USA: GSP suspended for Pakistan since 2013 — NOT ACTIVE
Canada: CARIBCAN does not apply — standard MFN rates
Australia: DFAT GSP — Pakistan eligible — limited benefits
Japan: GSP — Pakistan eligible
Norway, Switzerland: GSP — Pakistan eligible

EU GSP+ FOR PAKISTAN
---------------------
Status: ACTIVE — Pakistan is a GSP+ beneficiary country
Renewed: Every 10 years — currently valid through 2034
Conditions: Pakistan must ratify and implement 27 international conventions
            (labour rights, human rights, environment, governance)
Monitoring: European Commission monitors compliance annually

Benefits for Textile Exports:
- Cotton fabrics: 0% duty (MFN rate: 8%)
- Knitted fabrics: 0% duty (MFN rate: 8-12%)
- Garments: 0-9.6% duty (MFN rate: 12%)
- Home textiles (bed linen, towels): 0% duty (MFN rate: 12%)
- Savings example: EUR 100,000 CIF value at 12% = EUR 12,000 savings per shipment

Rules of Origin for EU GSP+:
- Textiles: "Double transformation" rule
  Stage 1: Fibre/yarn must be produced or substantially processed in Pakistan
  Stage 2: Fabric must be woven/knitted in Pakistan
  Stage 3: Garment must be cut and sewn in Pakistan
- Regional cumulation: SAARC countries (Bangladesh, India, Sri Lanka) can cumulate
- Proof of origin: REX (Registered Exporter) system OR Form A

REX System (Registered Exporter):
- Pakistan exporters must register in REX system
- Once registered: can issue Statement on Origin on invoice
- Statement on Origin replaces Form A for experienced exporters
- Registration: through TDAP and EU REX system

Form A for EU GSP+:
- Traditional proof of origin for GSP
- Issued by Chamber of Commerce in Pakistan
- Must be presented to EU customs by importer
- Origin criteria must be filled correctly

UK DCTS (Developing Countries Trading Scheme)
----------------------------------------------
Status: ACTIVE — Pakistan is Enhanced Framework beneficiary
Launched: June 2023 (replaced EU GSP post-Brexit)
Authority: UK Department for Business and Trade

Benefits:
- Cotton fabrics: 0-4% (MFN: 8-12%)
- Knitted fabrics: 0-4% (MFN: 12%)
- Garments: 0-9.6% (MFN: 12%)
- Home textiles: 0% (MFN: 12%)

Proof of Origin for UK DCTS:
- Form A (issued by Chamber of Commerce) OR
- Statement on Origin (for UK Approved Exporters)
- UK Approved Exporter: Apply through HMRC

GSP DUTY SAVINGS CALCULATION
------------------------------
Formula:
Standard Duty = CIF Value x MFN Duty Rate
GSP Duty = CIF Value x GSP Duty Rate
Saving = Standard Duty - GSP Duty

Example — Cotton Jersey Fabric to Germany:
CIF Value: USD 75,000
MFN Rate (EU): 8%
GSP+ Rate (EU): 0%
Standard Duty: USD 6,000
GSP+ Duty: USD 0
SAVING: USD 6,000 per shipment

Example — Garments to UK:
CIF Value: USD 55,000
MFN Rate (UK): 12%
DCTS Rate: 9.6%
Standard Duty: USD 6,600
DCTS Duty: USD 5,280
SAVING: USD 1,320 per shipment

RULES OF ORIGIN — KEY PRINCIPLES
----------------------------------
Wholly Obtained (WO): Goods entirely produced in Pakistan
  - Raw cotton grown in Pakistan → Cotton fabric → "WO"
  - Origin criterion on Form A: "P"

Sufficient Processing (SP): Goods processed enough to confer origin
  - Imported yarn + woven in Pakistan → "SP" if meets rule
  - Origin criterion on Form A: "W" + HS code change rule

Pakistan Textile — Common Origin Determination:
- 100% Pakistan cotton yarn + woven in Pakistan = "P" (Wholly Produced)
- Imported yarn + woven in Pakistan = "W" (check specific rule for HS code)
- Cut and sew garments (imported fabric) = generally NOT sufficient for GSP
- Cut, sew, finish (imported fabric) in Pakistan = check bilateral rules

FORM A PREPARATION GUIDE
--------------------------
Box 1: Goods consigned from (exporter name and address)
Box 2: Goods consigned to (consignee name and address)
Box 3: Means of transport and route
Box 4: For official use (leave blank — customs fills)
Box 5: Item number
Box 6: Marks and numbers on packages
Box 7: Number and kind of packages, description of goods
Box 8: Origin criterion (P or W)
Box 9: Gross weight (kg) or other quantity
Box 10: Invoice number and date
Box 11: Certification by competent authority (Chamber signature and stamp)
Box 12: Declaration by the exporter (exporter signature and date)

Critical Form A Rules:
- Description in Box 7 must match commercial invoice exactly
- Origin criterion must be correctly determined
- Chamber must sign and stamp Box 11
- No corrections or overwriting — prepare fresh if error
- Form A is valid for 10 months from issue date
