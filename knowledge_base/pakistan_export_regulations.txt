PAKISTAN EXPORT REGULATIONS FOR TEXTILE EXPORTERS
===================================================

REGULATORY BODIES
-----------------
1. TDAP — Trade Development Authority of Pakistan
   Role: Export promotion, certification, trade fair support
   Website: tdap.gov.pk
   Key services: Export certification, market access support

2. SBP — State Bank of Pakistan
   Role: Foreign exchange regulation, Form E management
   Key: All export proceeds must be repatriated within stipulated time

3. FBR — Federal Board of Revenue
   Role: Tax registration, customs clearance
   Key requirements: NTN (National Tax Number), STRN (Sales Tax Registration)

4. Pakistan Customs (under FBR)
   Role: Export clearance, GD (Goods Declaration) processing
   System: WeBOC (Web Based One Customs) — online GD filing

5. Chambers of Commerce
   FPCCI — Federation of Pakistan Chambers of Commerce and Industry
   KCCI — Karachi Chamber of Commerce and Industry
   LCCI — Lahore Chamber of Commerce and Industry
   FCCI — Faisalabad Chamber of Commerce and Industry
   Role: Certificate of Origin issuance, attestation

MANDATORY DOCUMENTS FOR PAKISTAN EXPORTS
-----------------------------------------

1. NTN — National Tax Number
   - Mandatory for all exporters
   - Issued by FBR
   - Must appear on all export documents
   - Register at: iris.fbr.gov.pk

2. STRN — Sales Tax Registration Number
   - Required for registered exporters
   - Zero-rated sales tax on exports
   - Must be on commercial invoice

3. Goods Declaration (GD)
   - Filed electronically on WeBOC system
   - Must be filed before goods reach port
   - Examiner may inspect goods physically
   - GD approval = permission to load

4. Form E — Export Form
   - SBP requirement for foreign exchange control
   - Filed with authorised dealer (bank) before shipment
   - Certifies export proceeds will be repatriated
   - Must match invoice value exactly
   - Repatriation period: 180 days from shipment date

5. EIF — Export Invoice Form
   - Required for invoices above USD 10,000
   - Bank endorsement required
   - Must match commercial invoice

CERTIFICATE OF ORIGIN PROCESS IN PAKISTAN
------------------------------------------
Step 1: Prepare commercial invoice (finalized)
Step 2: Apply to relevant Chamber of Commerce
   - KCCI for Karachi-based exporters
   - LCCI for Lahore-based exporters
   - FCCI for Faisalabad-based exporters
Step 3: Submit: Application form + Invoice + Packing List + GD copy
Step 4: Chamber verifies and issues CO (same day or next day)
Step 5: CO signed by Chamber official and stamped
Step 6: For GSP countries: Form A issued instead of standard CO
Step 7: For UAE/Saudi: CO requires Embassy attestation

Form A (Generalised System of Preferences)
- Used for exports to EU, UK, and other GSP-granting countries
- Must show: exporter name, consignee, origin criteria, gross weight
- Origin criteria for textiles: "P" (wholly produced) or "W" (sufficient working)
- Cotton fabric wholly produced in Pakistan = "P"
- Blended fabric using imported yarn = check rules of origin

TDAP REQUIREMENTS
-----------------
- Registration with TDAP for certain export categories
- TDAP issues: Export certificates, quota certificates (where applicable)
- Textile specific: TDAP maintains textile export statistics
- Pre-shipment inspection: Not mandatory for most textiles (removed 2021)

DCCI — DUTY AND TAX REMISSION FOR EXPORTERS
---------------------------------------------
DTRE (Duty and Tax Remission for Exports)
- Allows duty-free import of inputs for export production
- Must apply to Collector of Customs before import
- Bond required — cancelled upon export proof submission

DRAWBACK
- Customs duty drawback available on certain imported inputs
- Applied after export through WeBOC system

GST ZERO RATING
---------------
- All textile exports are zero-rated for GST
- Export invoice: Zero GST
- Input GST on purchases is refundable
- File GST refund claim through IRIS portal
- Refund typically processed within 60-90 days

PORT PROCEDURES — KARACHI
---------------------------
Port Qasim (QICT, PQICT):
- Main container terminal for textile exports
- Containers must be at port 48 hours before vessel cut-off
- GD must be approved before container enters port

Karachi Port Trust (KPT):
- Older terminal — still used for some shippers
- Similar procedures to Port Qasim

TIMING FOR DOCUMENTATION
-------------------------
D-7: Confirm vessel booking with freight forwarder
D-5: Finalize commercial invoice and packing list
D-4: Apply for Certificate of Origin from Chamber
D-3: File GD on WeBOC
D-2: Goods delivered to port / stuffing of container
D-1: GD examination and clearance
D-0: Vessel departure — BL issued within 24-48 hours
D+3: BL received — prepare full document set
D+7: Submit documents to bank (for LC) or courier to buyer (for TT)
