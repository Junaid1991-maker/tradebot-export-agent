TEXTILE CERTIFICATION GUIDE FOR EXPORT
=======================================

OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100
----------------------
Full Name: OEKO-TEX Standard 100 — Testing for Harmful Substances
Issuing Body: OEKO-TEX Association (Switzerland)
Website: oeko-tex.com

What it certifies:
- Every component of the textile product tested for harmful substances
- Covers: pesticides, heavy metals, formaldehyde, pH value, colour fastness
- Applies to: yarn, fabric, garments, accessories

Test Classes:
- Class I: Products for babies and toddlers (strictest)
- Class II: Products worn next to skin
- Class III: Products not in direct contact with skin (outerwear)
- Class IV: Furnishing materials

Certificate validity: 12 months — annual renewal required
Where to test in Pakistan:
- Intertek Pakistan (Karachi, Lahore, Faisalabad)
- SGS Pakistan
- Bureau Veritas Pakistan
- APTMA-affiliated testing labs

Which buyers require it:
- Most European buyers (Germany, France, Italy, UK) require OEKO-TEX
- USA buyers: increasingly required (Target, Walmart, H&M USA)
- UAE buyers: premium segment buyers
Cost: USD 500-2,000 depending on product complexity
Timeline: 4-8 weeks for initial certification

How to attach to export package:
- Include OEKO-TEX certificate copy in document package
- State certificate number on commercial invoice (optional but good practice)
- Buyer may verify online at oeko-tex.com

---

GOTS — GLOBAL ORGANIC TEXTILE STANDARD
---------------------------------------
Full Name: Global Organic Textile Standard
Issuing Body: Global Standard gGmbH (Germany)
Website: global-standard.org

What it certifies:
- Organic status of textile fibres (cotton, wool, linen)
- Processing standards: restricted substances in dyeing, finishing
- Social criteria: fair labour conditions
- Chain of custody: from farm to finished product

Requirements:
- Minimum 70% certified organic fibres (GOTS label "made with organic")
- Minimum 95% certified organic fibres (GOTS label)
- Entire supply chain must be GOTS certified

Where to certify in Pakistan:
- Control Union Pakistan
- OneCert International
- CCPB (Italian certifier operating in Pakistan)

Which buyers require it:
- Patagonia, H&M Conscious, IKEA organic range
- High-end EU buyers
- Any buyer claiming "organic" product

Cost: USD 800-3,000 per facility per year
Timeline: 3-6 months for initial certification
Important: Cannot claim GOTS without entire supply chain certified

How to attach to export package:
- GOTS transaction certificate per shipment (issued by certifier)
- Must accompany each organic shipment
- Certificate scope and products covered must match shipment

---

GRS — GLOBAL RECYCLED STANDARD
--------------------------------
Full Name: Global Recycled Standard
Issuing Body: Textile Exchange
Website: textileexchange.org

What it certifies:
- Recycled content in textile products
- Chain of custody for recycled materials
- Minimum 20% recycled content for GRS label use
- Social and environmental processing requirements

Applicable materials:
- Recycled polyester (rPET — from plastic bottles)
- Recycled cotton
- Recycled nylon

Where to certify in Pakistan:
- Control Union Pakistan
- Intertek Pakistan
- Bureau Veritas Pakistan

Which buyers require it:
- Primark (GRS mandatory for recycled claims)
- H&M, Zara (sustainability ranges)
- Nike, Adidas (recycled polyester products)
- Any buyer making recycled content claims

Cost: USD 600-2,000 per facility
Timeline: 2-4 months for certification

How to attach to export package:
- GRS transaction certificate per shipment
- Scope certificate of manufacturer
- Both documents typically required by buyer

---

REACH COMPLIANCE
-----------------
Full Name: Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals
Authority: European Chemicals Agency (ECHA)
Applicable to: All textile products sold in EU market

What it covers:
- Restriction of hazardous chemicals in textile articles
- SVHC (Substances of Very High Concern) — list updated regularly
- AZO dyes restriction (certain AZO dyes banned in EU)
- Formaldehyde limits
- Heavy metal limits (lead, cadmium, chromium VI)
- Nickel in metal accessories

Requirements for Pakistani exporters:
- Not a direct certification — but buyer compliance requirement
- Buyer typically requests: REACH compliance declaration OR test report
- Test report issued by accredited lab (Intertek, SGS, Bureau Veritas)
- Test standard: EN 14362 (AZO dyes) + REACH restricted substances list

How to attach to export package:
- REACH test report copy (from accredited lab)
- REACH compliance declaration (from exporter, based on test results)
- Valid for: 12 months typically — retest if formulation changes

---

ZDHC MRSL (MANUFACTURING RESTRICTED SUBSTANCE LIST)
-----------------------------------------------------
Full Name: Zero Discharge of Hazardous Chemicals — MRSL
Authority: ZDHC Foundation
Website: roadmaptozero.com

What it covers:
- Restricted chemicals used in textile manufacturing processes
- Dyestuffs, finishing chemicals, auxiliaries
- Applies to mills and wet processing units

Applicable to: Wet processing units, dye houses, finishing facilities
Pakistan relevance: Major buyers (H&M, PVH, Levi's) require suppliers to conform

How to demonstrate compliance:
- InCheck report (ZDHC platform) from chemical supplier
- Wastewater test report
- ZDHC Gateway — chemical compliance reporting

Attachment in export package:
- Not typically a shipment document
- Provided during buyer onboarding / supplier qualification
- Referenced in buyer's compliance declaration

CERTIFICATION REQUIREMENTS BY BUYER TYPE
------------------------------------------
Mass Market (Walmart, Target, Primark):
- OEKO-TEX Standard 100 (often mandatory)
- GRS (if recycled content claimed)
- REACH compliance declaration

Mid-Market (Next, Marks and Spencer, Dunelm):
- OEKO-TEX Standard 100
- GOTS (if organic claimed)
- REACH compliance declaration
- ZDHC MRSL conformance

Premium/Luxury (Armani, LVMH, Max Mara):
- OEKO-TEX Standard 100 or equivalent
- REACH compliance (full testing)
- Fabric composition testing report
- Country of origin documentation (very strict)

Sustainability Brands (Patagonia, H&M Conscious):
- GOTS (for organic) — mandatory
- GRS (for recycled) — mandatory
- ZDHC MRSL conformance
- Fair trade or equivalent social audit

Hospitality/Institutional (Hotels, Hospitals):
- OEKO-TEX Standard 100 (Class I or II)
- REACH compliance
- Colorfastness test report
- Specific performance test reports
