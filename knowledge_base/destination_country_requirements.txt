DESTINATION COUNTRY REQUIREMENTS FOR TEXTILE EXPORTS
======================================================

USA (United States of America)
-------------------------------
Required Documents:
- Commercial Invoice (must show HTS code, country of origin, buyer/seller details)
- Packing List (carton-wise breakdown mandatory)
- Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
- Certificate of Origin (Form A for GSP — USA suspended GSP for Pakistan as of 2013)
- Customs Bond (handled by US importer)
- Textile Visa / Export License (for quota categories if applicable)
- CPSC Compliance Certificate (for children's textile products)

Import Regulations:
- US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) governs all imports
- HTS (Harmonized Tariff Schedule) code must be declared
- Country of Origin must be clearly marked on goods
- Section 321 de minimis threshold: USD 800
- Lacey Act compliance for natural fiber products
- TSCA compliance for technical textiles

HS Code Format: 10-digit HTS code (first 6 digits international)
GSP Status: Pakistan GSP suspended by USA — no duty preference available
Standard Duty Rates: Cotton fabrics 7-12% | Knitwear 12-32% | Garments 12-32%

LC Requirements for USA Buyers:
- UCP 600 governs all LCs
- Documents must match LC terms exactly
- Typically requires: Commercial Invoice + Packing List + BL + CO

---

UK (United Kingdom)
--------------------
Required Documents:
- Commercial Invoice (GB EORI number of importer required)
- Packing List
- Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
- Certificate of Origin (Form A — UK GSP applies to Pakistan)
- UK Customs Declaration (handled by UK importer/agent)
- UKCA or CE marking (for applicable technical textiles)

Import Regulations:
- HMRC governs UK imports post-Brexit
- UK Global Tariff (UKGT) applies
- UK Generalised Scheme of Preferences (UKGSP) — Pakistan is eligible
- Enhanced Framework preference under UK DCTS
- Rules of Origin: substantial transformation in Pakistan required
- REACH equivalent regulations apply for chemical compliance

HS Code Format: 10-digit UK commodity code
GSP Status: Pakistan eligible for UK DCTS Enhanced Preferences
Duty Rates with GSP: Cotton fabrics 0-4% | Knitwear 0-12% | Garments 0-9.6%
Standard Duty Rates: Cotton fabrics 8-12% | Knitwear 12% | Garments 12%

---

GERMANY (European Union)
-------------------------
Required Documents:
- Commercial Invoice (must include EU buyer VAT number)
- Packing List
- Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
- EUR.1 Movement Certificate OR Statement on Origin (for GSP+)
- Certificate of Origin (Form A for GSP / GSP+)
- Customs Declaration SAD (Single Administrative Document)
- REACH Compliance Declaration (for chemical substances)
- EUDR Compliance (EU Deforestation Regulation — natural fibers)

Import Regulations:
- EU Customs Union applies — single entry point for all EU
- Combined Nomenclature (CN) 8-digit code required
- EU GSP+ applies to Pakistan — enhanced duty preferences
- Rules of Origin: sufficient processing in Pakistan
- REACH Regulation EC 1907/2006 mandatory
- EU Textile Labelling Regulation 1007/2011
- Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) — monitoring phase

HS Code Format: 8-digit CN code (EU Combined Nomenclature)
GSP Status: Pakistan eligible for EU GSP+ — significant duty reductions
Duty Rates GSP+: Cotton fabrics 0% | Knitwear 0% | Garments 0-9.6%
Standard Duty Rates: Cotton fabrics 8% | Knitwear 12% | Garments 12%

---

FRANCE (European Union)
------------------------
Same EU customs requirements as Germany.
Additional French-specific requirements:
- French language labelling may be required for retail products
- AGEC Law compliance (anti-waste — textile traceability)
- French customs code: same CN 8-digit as all EU

---

ITALY (European Union)
-----------------------
Same EU customs requirements as Germany.
Additional Italy-specific requirements:
- Made in Italy protection — country of origin must be clearly documented
- Italian buyers often require detailed fabric composition testing reports
- CONAI packaging compliance for Italian importers

---

UAE (United Arab Emirates)
---------------------------
Required Documents:
- Commercial Invoice (in English and Arabic preferred)
- Packing List
- Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
- Certificate of Origin (attested by Chamber of Commerce + Pakistan Embassy)
- Customs Declaration (handled by UAE clearing agent)
- Halal Certificate (if applicable for natural fiber products)
- Dubai Municipality approval (for certain textile chemicals)

Import Regulations:
- Federal Customs Authority (FCA) governs UAE imports
- GCC Common Customs Law applies
- Standard duty rate: 5% for most textiles
- No GSP — standard duty applies
- Country of Origin must be attested and authenticated
- Free Zone imports (Jebel Ali) have different regulations

HS Code Format: 6-digit HS code (GCC tariff)
GSP Status: None — standard 5% duty applies
Standard Duty Rates: Most textiles 5% | Some items 0%

---

SAUDI ARABIA
------------
Required Documents:
- Commercial Invoice (Arabic translation required)
- Packing List
- Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
- Certificate of Origin (authenticated by Chamber + Saudi Embassy)
- Saudi Standards (SASO) compliance certificate (for certain products)
- Customs Declaration

Import Regulations:
- Zakat Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA) governs
- GCC Common Customs Law applies
- Standard duty: 5-15% depending on product
- Saudization and Vision 2030 import policies
- SASO product conformity for regulated textile products
- Halal considerations for animal fiber products

HS Code Format: 6-digit HS code (GCC tariff)
GSP Status: None
Standard Duty Rates: Most textiles 5-15%

---

AUSTRALIA
---------
Required Documents:
- Commercial Invoice
- Packing List
- Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
- Certificate of Origin (self-declaration accepted for most)
- Import Declaration (handled by Australian importer)

Import Regulations:
- Australian Border Force (ABF) governs imports
- AIFTA (ASEAN-Australia-NZ FTA) does not include Pakistan
- No bilateral FTA with Pakistan — standard duty applies
- Biosecurity requirements for natural fiber products
- ACCC consumer product safety compliance

HS Code Format: 10-digit Australian Harmonized Code
GSP Status: Australia GSP (DFAT) — Pakistan eligible for some preferences
Standard Duty Rates: Most textiles 0-10%
